Chapter One

Some teachers teach lessons. You taught us how to learn.

Every page you opened for us turned into a door. You never just handed over the answers — you showed us how to look for them, and made us believe we were capable of finding them.

That kind of teaching doesn't end when the bell rings. It stays, quietly, in everything we do afterwards.

What you gave us

Four things no textbook could ever hold.

01

Patience

For explaining it a second time, and a third, without ever making us feel small for asking.

02

Wisdom

For lessons that reached far beyond the syllabus — the ones we only understood years later.

03

Kindness

For noticing the quiet ones, and for caring about who we were becoming, not just our grades.

04

Inspiration

For setting the bar high because you truly believed we could reach it — and then helping us do it.

A letter for you

Dear Sir,

Thank you for the patience when we were slow to understand, for the encouragement when we doubted ourselves, and for every time you asked more of us because you knew we had it in us.

A good teacher explains. A great teacher makes you believe you can.

You have always been the second kind. Whatever we go on to build in life, a part of it will always be yours.

With respect & gratitude Faisal Mehmood Awan

Sir Naveed Attari
